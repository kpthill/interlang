# The presentation script, in interlang

*Built 2026-08-06 for live delivery. **Lexicon frozen at the 19:15 snapshot** — background
agents were still rewriting `lexicon_milestone1.csv` while this was written, so forms shifted
twice mid-translation. Everything below is consistent with that snapshot and with nothing
later.*

**Real:** the grammar, exactly as decided (§3.2 of [`principles.md`](principles.md) — 20 words,
7 rules), and ~55 forms from the frozen lexicon.
**Provisional:** ~39 placeholder words in §2b, coined for this script. Unlike the first draft,
**these are deliberately spread across six families and contain no Romance at all** — the
first version was accidentally almost entirely Latin, which contradicted the very claim the
talk makes.

## 3. Honest limits

- **The 39 words in §2b are placeholders**, not optimizer output. They are phonotactically
  legal and collision-checked against the frozen lexicon, and nothing more. **If asked, say
  "those are placeholders."**
- **They are now family-spread rather than Romance**, which was the point of the rewrite — but
  they lean Austronesian and Atlantic-Congo, because Indonesian and Swahili have short
  CV-shaped words that survive our phonotactics unusually well. That is itself a mild bias and
  worth knowing: **our syllable template quietly favours donors that already look like it.**
- **The lexicon shifted twice while this was being written** (background agents mid-run).
  Forms here match the 19:15 snapshot only. `scripts/lexicon_milestone1.py` is a merge of two
  concurrent agent edits and must be reconciled before it is run again; the CSV is intact.
- **The grammar is exactly as decided** and can be defended construction by construction.
- Simplifications, recorded so English and interlang do not silently diverge: "regardless of
  their first language" → *their first language is not special*; "kinda sucks" → *very similar
  to European languages*; the loanword sentence split in two; "ran out of time" → *I do not
  have time*.
